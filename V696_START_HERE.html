PICU HANDOFF / VERSION 6.96

IV fluids and visible processing progress

Recognized continuous IV fluids appear under Intravenous Fluids in FEN/GI. The rate is compared with hourly 4-2-1 maintenance using the documented dosing weight.

Rates at or below 2 mL/hr stay visible without a maintenance comparison. Missing or failed dosing-weight information also leaves the comparison blank.

Progress while you wait

A spinner shows elapsed time while the app reads the SmartPhrase, saves the handoff and prepares its PDF. Later imports show typical remaining time based on recent successful imports in the same session.

Install from 6.95

Extract PICU696.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_96_IV_Fluids_Progress. Copy these seven files into the existing app folder:

app.py
categories.py
error_store.py
fen_gi.py
infusions.py
iv_fluids.py
processing_feedback.py

The last two files are new and must be included beside app.py.

Reboot Streamlit once and confirm Version 6.96.

For an earlier app version, use the complete package contents. Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets and encryption key.
