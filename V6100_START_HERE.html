PICU Handoff 6.100

Automatic vecuronium and rocuronium PRN counts.

Install

PICU Handoff 6.100
Baseline: 6.99.2

Extract PICU6100.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_100_Automatic_Paralytic_Counts.

Replace these seven files beside the app.py that Streamlit runs:
app.py
categories.py
error_store.py
exports.py
importing.py
prn_counts.py
prn_html.py

Commit the replacements and reboot Streamlit once.
Confirm Version 6.100 in the sidebar.
Paste the complete SmartPhrase as usual. Paralytic counts are automatic.
There is no PRN review form or confirmation step.

Only vecuronium and rocuronium are counted. Each positive dated volume entry
contributes one count, with exact duplicate entries counted once.
Analgesic and sedative entries are ignored.
The count is still shown when a paralytic infusion is also present.
The window starts at the most recent 7 a.m. Eastern and ends at print time.
The count appears after PRN in Neurologic, without administration dates or times.

The same seven-file update works from 6.99 and 6.99.1.
For 6.98 or earlier, use the complete package's application files.
Keep the existing Streamlit Secrets and encryption key.

462 targeted tests passed. Dependencies and archive schema 34 are unchanged.
Ready to install. No live deployment was performed.
