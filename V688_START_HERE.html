The entire login view now clears together before saved handoffs begin loading. This prevents the password form from briefly appearing without its centered-card styling.

The password, encryption key, retry delay, session limits and logout behavior are unchanged. No extra delay or rerun has been added. Updating from 6.87 requires only the two files listed above, followed by a Streamlit restart.

Lysis Labs in Hematology

The included lysis_labs.py module remains beside app.py. HGBFREE, LDH and uric acid results from section G now appear under a bold Lysis Labs: heading at the top of Hematology. Each available test has its own line.

Lysis Labs:
HGBFREE: <8 [11/20/2026]
LDH: 180 -> 220 [11/20/2026]
Uric acid: 4.8 -> 6.0 [11/20/2026]

This synthetic example shows the format. One result has no arrow. With more results, the two latest eligible values are displayed older to newer. Missing tests are omitted. The date at the end is the date of the newest selected result.

The window follows lactate: rolling 24 hours against the current Eastern time, including the existing date-only overlap rule. The latest two values are used even when more than two are available. Original values, units and flags are preserved. Lysis results appear in Hematology without a second copy in lower Labs.

ECLS in CV

The paired adadadad section supplies the circuit settings. ECLS remains below CV vitals and cardiac labs, before notes and medications. Each setting has its own line, with no pipe separators.

This synthetic example uses dosing weight 20 kg and reported BSA 0.8 m². The six logical lines include the heading. Long lines can wrap within the existing column. Missing settings or calculation inputs show UNK.

AED Drug Levels

The latest valid measured PHENOBARB and PHENYTOIN results are selected independently. Results within the past 24 hours, including the exact boundary, appear under the bold AED Drug Levels: heading in Neuro. Neuro retains separate lines for its heading and each drug. Older results appear in lower Labs on one continuous line with their original Last checked dates. The line wraps naturally if needed.

A synthetic lower Labs example, with the heading and results inline:

AED Drug Levels: Phenobarbital: 30.2 [Last checked: 11/19/2026], Phenytoin: 7.1 (L) [Last checked: 11/18/2026 12:00]

A recent result for one drug can appear in Neuro while the other drug stays in Labs. Each drug appears once. Its values, flags and source dates are preserved. Units are not invented, and free Phenytoin is not combined with total Phenytoin.

The window uses the current Eastern time. A date without a time qualifies for Neuro only when the beginning of that date is within the past 24 hours. At 08:00 on November 20, a result dated only November 19 therefore stays in Labs with its recorded date.

A complete replacement paste without G clears prior drug levels for that room. A replacement paste without AD clears prior ECLS settings. Other rooms stay separate.
