Both paralytic counts always start at 7 a.m. on the previous Eastern calendar
day. Before 7 a.m. today, count through the current print time. At 7 a.m. or
later, cap the count at the end of today's 6:59 a.m. minute. Today's doses at
7 a.m. or later are excluded. The count does not restart at 7 a.m.

Examples for a handoff printed today:
4 a.m. print: yesterday 7 a.m. through today 4 a.m.
8 a.m. print: yesterday 7 a.m. through today 6:59 a.m.

Vecuronium and rocuronium counts are automatic. No review or confirmation is
required. Only the number is added after PRN. Analgesics and sedatives are not
counted, and a paralytic infusion does not suppress the count.
