Re-paste the complete SmartPhrase to refresh measurements and record the
date of the supplied general Age. Alternatively, enter HC Age for a saved HC.

HC percentile is shown in the patient column after the measurement date.
WHO age/sex-specific reference values are bundled locally. A dated HC uses
the documented Age and its captured date to calculate age at measurement.
The ~ marker identifies an estimate based on an age without day precision.
An explicit HC Age overrides this fallback and may contain a clinician-selected
corrected age when appropriate. Whole-year age alone does not select an HC
percentile. No birth date is requested or stored.

Optional SmartPhrase examples:
HC Age: 183 days
HC Age: 6 months
Head Circumference Age: 26 weeks 1 day

Estimated IBW appears for ages 2 through 19 when recorded BMI is at or above
the CDC 95th percentile for age and sex and a usable height is available.
IBW = CDC median BMI for age and sex times height in meters squared.
The result is labeled as an estimate and does not automatically replace the
measured or dosing weight in medication, fluid, nutrition or ventilation work.

The app reads existing archive schema 34 records. New optional hc_age and
age_as_of values are raw age provenance, not stored derived results. All
runtime files above must be deployed together. No dependency, Secrets or
encryption-key change is required.
