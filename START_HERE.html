PICU Handoff 6.63

Chemotherapy and clean medication labels

Recognized chemotherapy drugs now appear under Hematology > Chemotherapy. Dexamethasone is excluded from this new rule. Existing explicit group and category choices remain available.

Leading And:, Or: and [START ON ...] annotations are hidden in the displayed medication name. Stored orders, doses, routes, frequencies and scheduling data stay intact. Held and discontinued labels remain visible.

The latest supplied SmartPhrase imports all 35 medication orders. See the supported chemotherapy names and the synthetic printed example.

Choose how to start after sign-in

Start new patient list shows a review of the rooms to remove and keep. Confirm to remove older handoffs and today's handoffs last saved from 5:00 AM through 8:30 AM Eastern, then enter your patients.

Continue saved handoffs keeps the existing list so you can print processed handoffs, update patients or add patients. Signing in and choosing Continue do not delete anything.

Today's handoffs saved outside the morning window stay. Missing, invalid or future save times are kept for review. After the first successful start, later sign-ins keep that day's list and cannot repeat the daily cleanup.

Cleared handoffs include their difficult-airway details. The keep-airway reset option and airway Keep/Delete pop-up are removed. The normal airway editor remains.

Upload the complete folder so the new chemotherapy.py module and all related formatting changes are included. Restart Streamlit and refresh the browser.

Included features

  • Medication imports recognize Or, And and Followed by. Linked alternatives retain their source order within a category.
  • TMax remains regular text in the preview and PDF.
  • MRSA Screen: appears in demographics, with a bold label and the newest supplied result across MRSADNA and MRSANPADM, selected by the laboratory collection date and time.
  • Bupivacaine orders appear once under Neuro > Epidural, preserving their documented dose, route and rate.
  • Hypernatremia appears in Problems when the latest usable blood sodium is above 145.
  • Vascular lines keep their insertion date and gain the current Day number. Insertion is Day 1, using Eastern calendar dates.

Install

Upload this folder's contents to your existing code repository, keeping app.py at the root. Include chemotherapy.py, daily_start.py, census_guard.py and all earlier modules. Restart the app and refresh your browser.

Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets, passwords, encryption key, mappings, custom categories, BMI reference data and shared archive. Do not rerun setup generators. No new dependencies or archive-schema changes are needed.

Save-before-Print-All, per-room saved timestamps and conflict checks remain in place. PDF downloads check for a shared patient-list reset before releasing a new file.

Source data and printing

MRSADNA and MRSANPADM are read from the G laboratory block. Each result needs its laboratory collection date. The newest result across both codes is shown. Existing saved results, orders and line dates gain the new displays immediately. Paste a fresh SmartPhrase to add data that was not previously included.

The preview and new downloads calculate current line days. Previously downloaded PDFs remain fixed snapshots.
