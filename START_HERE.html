PICU Handoff 6.83

Dedicated invasive NAVA settings, built on your version 6.80 baseline.

Install the update

Replace these files together at the root of your existing app repository, beside app.py:

app.py
ventilation.py
noninvasive.py

Restart Streamlit. The sidebar should read Version 6.83 | Invasive NAVA settings.

No new dependency or configuration change is required. Keep your current Secrets, encryption keys, archives, and mappings.

Use your current SmartPhrase

When Vent Mode is Neurally Adjusted Ventilatory Assist, the app reads the NAVA Ventilator Settings subsection. Paste the handoff again after installing to populate these fields. Your supplied example needs no changes.

README.md includes the output example. TEST_REPORT.md records verification and ARCHIVE_COMPATIBILITY.txt describes saved-file compatibility.
