Compact IV fluid entries

Extract PICU697.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_97_Compact_IV_Fluids. Replace these files in your app folder:

app.py
error_store.py
infusions.py
iv_fluids.py

Reboot Streamlit once and confirm Version 6.97.

For an earlier version, use the complete package contents. Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets and encryption key.
