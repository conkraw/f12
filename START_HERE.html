PICU Handoff 6.60

What changed

TMax now uses regular text in the preview and PDF. From 6.59, replace app.py and clinical_style.py together, then restart Streamlit.

Included features

  • MRSA Screen: appears in demographics, with a bold label and the newest supplied result across MRSADNA and MRSANPADM, selected by the laboratory collection date and time.
  • Bupivacaine orders appear once under Neuro > Epidural, preserving their documented dose, route and rate.
  • Hypernatremia appears in Problems when the latest usable blood sodium is above 145.
  • Vascular lines keep their insertion date and gain the current Day number. Insertion is Day 1, using Eastern calendar dates.

Install

Upload this folder's contents to your existing code repository, keeping app.py at the root. Include the new mrsa.py file. Restart the app and refresh your browser.

Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets, passwords, encryption key, mappings, custom categories, BMI reference data and shared archive. Do not rerun setup generators. No new dependencies or archive-schema changes are needed.

The existing save-before-Print-All behavior, per-room saved timestamps and difficult-airway workflow remain in place.

Source data and printing

MRSADNA and MRSANPADM are read from the G laboratory block. Each result needs its laboratory collection date. The newest result across both codes is shown. Existing saved results, orders and line dates gain the new displays immediately. Paste a fresh SmartPhrase to add data that was not previously included.

The preview and new downloads calculate current line days. Previously downloaded PDFs remain fixed snapshots.

See README.md for details and the synthetic example for the printed layout.
