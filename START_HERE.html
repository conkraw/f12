PICU Handoff 6.84

Centered sign in and compact ECLS settings in the CV section, built on version 6.83.

Install

Copy the contents of the extracted folder into the root of your existing app repository, beside app.py. Replace matching files and add the new modules.

For an update from 6.83, upload these files together:

app.py
login_ui.py
ecls.py
parser.py
importing.py
models.py
import_review.py
exports.py
clinical_style.py
batching.py
error_store.py

Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets and encryption key. No new dependencies or configuration are needed.

Restart Streamlit. Confirm the sidebar reads Version 6.84 | ECLS settings and centered sign in.

ECLS in CV

Re-paste the current complete SmartPhrase. The paired adadadad section supplies the circuit settings. ECLS appears under CV vitals and cardiac labs, before notes and medications.

ECLS settings:
Mode: VA [Oxy Day: 0]
Blood flow: 2.3 L/min, 85.2 mL/kg/min [CI: 2.3 L/min/m²]
RPM: 3400 | Sweep: 1.2 L/min | FiO₂: 50%

This example uses dosing weight 27 kg and reported BSA 1 m². Missing settings or calculation inputs show UNK. A replacement paste without ECLS clears the prior room's ECLS settings.

README.md includes field mapping and calculations. TEST_REPORT.md describes verification.
