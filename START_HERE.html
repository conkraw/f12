PICU HANDOFF / VERSION 6.94

Save your work and go Home

Home is the first button in the left sidebar. It saves open handoff edits and returns to the original starting screen while you stay signed in.

From there, choose to continue with the current list or start a fresh one. The existing confirmation still appears before patients are cleared.

If saving fails, the app keeps you in the workspace with your edits available.

Install from 6.93

Extract PICU694.zip. Copy app.py and error_store.py from PICU_Handoff_v6_94_Home_Button into your existing app folder.

Reboot Streamlit once and confirm Version 6.94. After that, Home takes users back to the beginning without restarting the website.

For an earlier app version, use the complete package contents. Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets and encryption key.
