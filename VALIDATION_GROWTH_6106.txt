PICU Handoff 6.106 validation

Base: conkraw/f12 version 6.105
Base commit: 2c4095ff27a869d15f577c53ae562a48c7f8d905

The selected regression suite passed 1,077 tests.
It covered the new BMI Age parser and age resolver, calculation and category
boundaries, existing demographics and laboratory imports, replacement imports,
Problems review and dismissal, encrypted saved records, editor callbacks,
screen rendering, all handoff export formats, and PDF cache invalidation.
UI checks use the project's widget stubs, not a deployed browser.

New BMI Age behavior checked:
- BMI Age: 2 y.o. 1 m.o. resolves to 25 months and leaves Age: 2 y.o. unchanged.
- The BMI z score, percentile and high-BMI category use the same age and sex.
- Standalone, next-line and inline metadata forms import correctly.
- Explicit incomplete or contradictory BMI Age never falls back silently.
- A successful new Nutrition import clears an omitted previous BMI Age.
- Old five-field records still validate and round-trip through encryption.
- Editing BMI Age refreshes exports and relevant Problems review decisions.

Calculation and Problems checks:
- Male, BMI 11.9, BMI Age 25 months produces z -5.73426217561093.
  The handoff displays -5.73 (CDC) and Severe malnutrition in Problems.
- Overweight begins at the unrounded LMS P85, obesity at the published P95,
  and severe obesity at 120% of P95 or BMI 35 kg/m2, whichever is lower.
- Only the highest applicable high-BMI category is returned.
- High-BMI classification remains available if an extreme positive z score
  cannot be represented by the normal-tail calculation.
- Children under 2 retain WHO WFL scoring. A WFL z above +2 adds
  High weight-for-length. BMI Age cannot change infant chart eligibility.
- Display rounding preserves the malnutrition and high-WFL boundaries.
- Age 20 and older does not receive a pediatric BMI z score or category.

Layout checks:
Three dense handoffs were rendered to PDF and inspected as PNG images.
Each remained one page without clipping the new BMI Age, z score or Problems.
The cases were the earlier infant source, a synthetic dense case using the
supplied 25-month BMI values, and a synthetic severe-obesity variant.
The infant retained its dated head-circumference line. The older cases omitted
head circumference. The untyped source weight-chart z was not printed.
Patient source pastes and generated PDFs/images are not in this package.

The WHO and CDC public reference tables are unchanged from 6.105. Their source
URLs and checksums remain in data/GROWTH_REFERENCE_SOURCES.txt. This release
adds classification and age routing, without replacing those validated tables.

Python compilation and git whitespace checks passed.
The suite completed with pypdf deprecation warnings.
No live Streamlit deployment was changed or verified during this build.
