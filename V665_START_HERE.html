PICU Handoff 6.65

Mapping Sync and the montelukast respiratory group.

Update each application

Upload this folder's contents to each app-code repository, keeping app.py at its root. Restart Streamlit and confirm Version 6.65. Keep all existing passwords, Secrets, encryption keys and patient archives.

Connect your mapping repositories

Read MAPPING_SYNC_SETUP.md. Append the two other private data repositories to your existing Streamlit Secrets using MAPPING_SYNC_SECRETS.example.toml. Your current data repository is included automatically.

Review before saving

Open Admin, unlock it, and expand Mapping Sync. Compare the saved mappings, resolve conflicts, inspect the proposed rules, and approve the merge. There is no automatic synchronization.

Patient records and mappings share one encrypted archive. Only mapping settings are synchronized. Each destination retains its own patient data. Encrypted mapping backups are created before mapping writes. A partial failure is reported per repository without a whole-archive rollback.

Montelukast

The new automatic category is Respiratory > Leukotriene receptor antagonist. Explicit custom assignments remain available. No medication dose or source value changes.
