PICU Handoff 6.87 archive compatibility

Baseline: delivered version 6.86.
The archive schema remains 34. No data migration is required.
Dependencies, configuration and encryption requirements are unchanged.

Lysis Labs is derived for display from the existing G-section laboratory rows.
No separate lysis archive field or cached selection is stored. Original source
components, values, units, flags and dates remain unchanged. The current
Eastern display or print clock determines the rolling 24-hour window.

Saved handoffs with supported HGBFREE, LDH or uric acid rows can use the new
Hematology display immediately. Re-paste a current complete SmartPhrase to
update the underlying results. The existing full replacement workflow clears
prior results when a new room paste omits G and preserves other rooms.

Version 6.86 can read the same archive fields but does not provide the new
Hematology lysis trends. The centered login, ECLS, AED and NAVA data formats
remain compatible with their respective earlier versions. Versions before
6.84 do not recognize the optional ECLS field or AD review flags.

No patient identifiers, source patient paste or production credentials are
added to this delivery package.
