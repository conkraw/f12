PICU Handoff 6.84 archive compatibility

Baseline: version 6.83, including its invasive NAVA changes.
The archive schema remains 34. Existing archives open without a migration.

ECLS is an optional structured field on a handoff. Legacy handoffs can omit it.
Only the six normalized ECLS settings are stored. Derived indexed flow and CI
are recomputed from the current settings, dosing weight and recorded BSA.
Raw AD source narrative is not saved.

Versions before 6.84 do not recognize the optional ECLS field or AD review
flags. Use version 6.84 or later for archives that contain ECLS. A rollback
requires an archive compatible with the chosen older application version.

The existing NAVA, encrypted storage and mapping formats remain in place.
Re-paste a complete current SmartPhrase to populate ECLS in older handoffs.
