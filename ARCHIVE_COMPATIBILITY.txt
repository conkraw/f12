Version 6.62 accepts existing schema 34 archives without rewriting them.

The optional settings.patient_list_started_at field stores the aware timestamp
of the first confirmed daily start. Its Eastern date prevents another daily
reset on the same date. The marker, selected removals and reset_generation are
committed together. Opening the app or choosing Continue does not add the marker.

The existing encryption, dependencies and stored patient-sheet structure are
unchanged. All settings and unselected saved handoffs remain intact during a
daily cleanup. Selected handoffs are deleted completely, including airway data.
There is no airway-only carry-forward record.

Use the full 6.62 application after the marker has been saved. Earlier versions
do not recognize this additional setting and will reject that newer archive.
No setup generator or encryption-key change is needed.

Keep existing Streamlit Secrets, encryption key and shared archive.
