PICU Handoff 6.83 archive compatibility

Based on version 6.80. The archive schema remains 34.
Existing archives can be opened without a migration. Encryption and storage code are unchanged.

Five ventilation field labels are appended: NAVA level, EDI peak, EDI min,
Resp Rate Observed, and Apnea Interval (sec). Existing field indices and the
mode/values structure are unchanged.

Older app versions do not recognize these new saved fields. Use version 6.83
or later for archives containing the new NAVA fields. When testing a rollback,
use a compatible pre-update archive rather than asking an older app to read
new NAVA records.

Re-paste the current SmartPhrase to populate fields that were not retained by
older versions. Missing fields are not reconstructed from historical values.
