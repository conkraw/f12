Update all three apps to v6.66 before synchronizing those mapping values.

archive schema number has not changed.

Read-only checks and comparison do not migrate, repair or save archives.

changes only the mapping allowlist, and uses its current GitHub SHA.

remain those of that destination. Conflict review and encrypted mapping-only
backups remain required. Cross-repository writes are not atomic.
