PICU Handoff 6.103 archive compatibility

Archive schema remains 34. Existing archives remain readable by version 6.103.
Dependencies, credentials, encryption keys, and repository settings are
unchanged. No archive migration is required.

Flow-table mode entries use the existing ventilation history structure.
The newest mode becomes primary. Earlier table modes retain their timestamps
and start unchecked in the existing history editor. The older individual
SmartLink format keeps its existing display behavior.

A timestamped unknown primary mode may now be retained with an empty settings
dictionary. No settings are borrowed from a known older mode. This newly
accepted record may require the updated reader if the app is downgraded.
Install the 6.103 runtime files together.

Version 6.102 AF-table parsing and PRN records are preserved. The optional PRN
record keeps its version 1 shape, timestamps, source volumes, dose counts, and
legacy review metadata. Older eight-drug records remain readable, but only
vecuronium and rocuronium receive automatic counts. Legacy review decisions
do not gate those counts. New imports retain only the two paralytics.

The count window still runs from 07:00 on the previous Eastern calendar day
through preparation or printing. Five qualifying entries display as PRN x 5.
Six or more display as PRN >=6. Administration timestamps are preserved.

Existing archives without PRN data remain valid. Mapping-only synchronization
preserves patient data locally and does not copy PRN records. Complete room
replacement and the encrypted-save, conflict, and download guards are unchanged.
No supplied patient examples are included in this release.
