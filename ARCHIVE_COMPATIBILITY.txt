PICU Handoff v6.71 archive compatibility

Schema remains 34. Existing archives open without migration. No saved field
is removed. The diet rows, enteral and parenteral lists, recorded_feed_ids,
comments, additives and original source remain stored as in v6.70.
Calculated display amounts are not saved as source data.

Existing v6.70 records display in the compact format after installing v6.71.
Structured nutrition records and AB diagnostics still require v6.70 or later.
Use v6.71 to retain the new presentation, calorie calculations and duration
rules. Older releases do not reproduce these display choices.

security.py, github_store.py, mapping_sync.py, mapping_sync_ui.py,
sync_diagnostics.py, models.py, exports.py and requirements.txt match v6.70.
No encryption key, repository path or Secrets field changes. Each app retains
its separate encrypted data repository and current medication mappings.
