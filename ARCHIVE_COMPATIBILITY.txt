PICU Handoff 6.80 archive compatibility

Archive schema remains 34. Encryption and the patient-data storage format
are unchanged. No key rotation, storage reset or data migration is required.

The new Airway Clearance category is backfilled for older category catalogs.
Existing category definitions and their original capacity remain available.
Source names, doses, routes and frequencies are preserved.

Calculated per-dose annotations exist only on display copies. They are not
written into medication records or saved mapping rules.

Generic sodium chloride/NaCl rules targeting Respiratory no longer sweep
nonqualifying sodium orders into that section. More specific assignments
remain available. The saved settings list itself is not deleted.

Install the complete runtime update listed in UPDATE_FILES.txt.
Keep existing Streamlit Secrets and encrypted patient/mapping storage.

The 6.79 session-private PDF cache and 6.78 complete-room replacement
workflow are retained. Cache signatures include dose, weight and current
display. Old downloads and printed pages cannot be recalled.
