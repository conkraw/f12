WHO weight-for-recumbent-length reference
Retrieved 2026-11-22

The bundled who_wfl_lms.csv contains the complete sex-specific L, M and S
parameters from the WHO Child Growth Standards expanded weight-for-length
tables, 45.0 to 110.0 cm in 0.1 cm increments. There are 651 rows per sex.
Sex 1 is male. Sex 2 is female. Measurements use centimeters and kilograms.
The CSV copies the first four columns of each official Excel table and adds
the Sex column. No parameter values are estimated or fabricated.

WHO calculation:
For L != 0, z = ((weight / M)^L - 1) / (L * S).
For L = 0, z = ln(weight / M) / S.
Within -3 <= z <= 3, retain the LMS z score.
Below -3, z = -3 + (weight - weight_at_minus3) /
                           (weight_at_minus2 - weight_at_minus3).
Above +3, z = 3 + (weight - weight_at_plus3) /
                          (weight_at_plus3 - weight_at_plus2).
Use full precision for classification. Round only for display.

The caller limits this app's WFL assessment to patients under 24 months.
The calculation needs recumbent length. Do not silently use standing height
or adjust a measurement unless its actual measurement position is known.
The module makes no age inference and does not calculate weight-for-age.
No patient measurements or identifiers are stored in these reference files.

CDC BMI-for-age reference is documented in BMI_REFERENCE.md.
