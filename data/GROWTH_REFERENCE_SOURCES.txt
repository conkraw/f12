WHO weight-for-recumbent-length reference
Retrieved 2026-11-22

The bundled who_wfl_lms.csv contains the complete sex-specific L, M and S
parameters from the WHO Child Growth Standards expanded weight-for-length
tables, 45.0 to 110.0 cm in 0.1 cm increments. There are 651 rows per sex.
Sex 1 is male. Sex 2 is female. Measurements use centimeters and kilograms.
The CSV copies the first four columns of each official Excel table and adds
the Sex column. No parameter values are estimated or fabricated.

WHO calculation:
For L != 0, z = ((weight / M)^L - 1) / (L * S).
For L = 0, z = ln(weight / M) / S.
Within -3 <= z <= 3, retain the LMS z score.
Below -3, z = -3 + (weight - weight_at_minus3) /
                           (weight_at_minus2 - weight_at_minus3).
Above +3, z = 3 + (weight - weight_at_plus3) /
                          (weight_at_plus3 - weight_at_plus2).
Use full precision for classification. Round only for display.

The caller limits this app's WFL assessment to patients under 24 months.
The calculation needs recumbent length. Do not silently use standing height
or adjust a measurement unless its actual measurement position is known.
The module makes no age inference and does not calculate weight-for-age.
No patient measurements or identifiers are stored in these reference files.

WHO head circumference-for-age reference (version 6.108)
Retrieved 2026-11-24

The bundled who_hcfa_lms.csv contains the exact daily L, M and S strings
from the official WHO anthro hcanthro.txt data. Sex 1 is male and 2 is female.
There are 1827 rows per sex, days 0 through 1826. HC measurements use cm.
The app displays HC for patients under 2 years only.

The conversion renames columns to Sex,AgeDays,L,M,S and sorts by sex/day.
No parameters are estimated. WHO day rounding uses floor(age_days + 0.5).
WHO month conversion uses 30.4375 days per month. HC uses age at measurement.
The daily HC table has L=1 throughout. z = ((HC/M)^L - 1)/(L*S).
Percentile = 100 times the standard normal cumulative probability of z.
Only reference data is cached. Percentiles are calculated from current inputs.

Pediatric estimated ideal body weight (version 6.108)

Trigger: recorded BMI at or above the unrounded CDC age/sex-specific 95th
percentile, for 24 <= age_months < 240. The existing CDC BMI data supplies
P95 and median M. The same monthly bin is used for eligibility and estimate.
Estimated IBW kg = median BMI M times recorded height in meters squared.

CDC weight categories:
https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/child-teen-calculator/bmi-categories.html

Original study describing and comparing pediatric IBW methods:
Kang K, et al. Evaluation of Different Methods Used to Calculate Ideal Body
Weight in the Pediatric Population. J Pediatr Pharmacol Ther.
2019 Sep-Oct, 24(5):421-430. DOI: 10.5863/1551-6776-24.5.421.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6782117/

There is no universal pediatric IBW gold standard. The app labels this as a
reference estimate. Existing measured-weight and dosing-weight calculations
are not replaced, including medication, fluid, nutrition and ventilation use.
The broad 45 to 250 cm height check is a likely-unit-error entry guard, not
a clinical reference range or validation of an individual child's proportions.
