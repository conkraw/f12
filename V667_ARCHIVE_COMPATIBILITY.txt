are retained. Older first-colon-split arterial vital rows are interpreted only
for presentation, without altering their stored values.

sync_diagnostics.py and requirements.txt match the supplied v6.66 package.

are not changed. No migration or additional Streamlit Secrets are needed.

All three app-code repositories can use this package with their existing,
separate data repositories. No patient recall data is copied between them.
