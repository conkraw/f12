PICU Handoff v6.65 archive compatibility

Archive schema remains 34. Existing schema-34 patient archives remain valid.
Normal app loading retains existing migration behavior for older archives.
Mapping Sync itself refuses older or unknown schemas and does not migrate them.

New built-in category: group:leukotriene_receptor_antagonist
Section: Respiratory
Label: Leukotriene receptor antagonist
Older category catalogs expose the built-in without altering saved definitions.
All 250 original category slots remain available, plus the three backfilled
Epidural, Chemotherapy and Leukotriene receptor antagonist built-ins.

Install v6.65 on every app before saving or synchronizing the new group mode.
Older application validators may reject a new explicit medication-group value.

Only allowlisted mapping fields are replaced during synchronization. Patient
records, saved source medications, notes, room lists, diagnosis settings,
reset-generation values and daily-list timestamps are preserved per repository.
Hidden-medication preferences are preserved unless explicitly included.

Mapping-only backups have their own format. They are not handoff archives.
Do not replace the main encrypted archive with a mapping backup.
