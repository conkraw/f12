PICU Handoff version 6.106
BMI Age and growth categories

INSTALL

This update builds on the installed conkraw/f12 version 6.105.

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open conkraw/f12 in GitHub and use Add file > Upload files.
3. Drag in the extracted files and folders. Replace the Python files and keep
   the data folder intact, including its two growth reference CSV files.
4. Commit together, reboot Streamlit, confirm Version 6.106, and re-paste each
   patient's complete SmartPhrase.

The package includes the required modules and both reference tables. Do not
upload only app.py. No new secrets or requirements.txt change is needed.

BMI AGE

The new field is imported directly:
BMI Age: 2 y.o. 1 m.o.

That means 25 months. It is used for the BMI-for-age z score, BMI percentile,
and high-BMI category. General Age: 2 y.o. stays unchanged in the patient header.
BMI Age is displayed with the BMI measurements on the handoff.

Other accepted examples include BMI Age: 25 months and BMI Age: 2 years 1 month.
The field can be edited under Labs / Vitals > Demographics / Weight.

BMI Age takes priority when present. Without it, a precise general age in
months or years plus months can still be used. Missing or invalid age precision
does not produce a guessed score. A new Nutrition import clears any prior
BMI Age omitted from the new source.

EXAMPLE RESULT

Male growth-chart sex, recorded BMI 11.9, BMI Age 2 y.o. 1 m.o.:
BMI-for-age z score: -5.73 (CDC)
Problems: Severe malnutrition

The score printed beside the Epic weight reading is not used for this result.

NEW PROBLEMS

Ages 2 through 19:
Overweight: BMI at or above P85 and below P95.
Obesity: BMI at or above P95, unless severe criteria apply.
Severe obesity: BMI at or above 120% of P95, or BMI at or above 35 kg/m².

Only the highest applicable high-BMI category is added. These are the CDC
categories associated with the age- and sex-specific BMI distribution.
Severe obesity is not assigned from an arbitrary fixed z-score threshold.

Below age 2:
High weight-for-length is added when WHO WFL z is above +2. Infant obesity
labels are not applied. Existing mild, moderate and severe malnutrition rules
are unchanged. Normal growth does not add a problem.

All labels use the existing Problems review/hide controls. Changes to the
supporting BMI, BMI Age, sex, weight or length refresh the calculation and
relevant review decision. Age 20 and older remains outside pediatric scoring.

PRIOR UPDATE FEATURES

Head circumference below age 2 and abdominal girth when supplied retain their
latest measurement dates. The age-appropriate z score is in the demographics
column. WFL continues to use measured weight and recumbent length. Dosing
weight is not used for growth classification. The previous ventilator,
paralytic-table and airway-clearance updates remain included.

BMI_REFERENCE.md explains the calculation and classification methods.
VALIDATION_GROWTH_6106.txt describes the checks performed for this build.
