Version 6.21 | Unified DVT Prophylaxis group

Heparin and cangrelor infusions stay in the same DVT Prophylaxis group as
enoxaparin and other orders assigned to this category. The infusion medication
name remains bold, and its ordered dose stays bold and underlined. A different
recorded pump rate appears separately. No extra Heme Infusions heading is
printed for these DVT orders. Non-infusion entries keep their compact format.
Other unrelated hematology infusions may still use a Heme Infusions heading.

The automatic group rules and manual choices are unchanged. Hidden and excluded
orders remain hidden. Each order appears once. Source medication fields, rates,
notes, saved mappings, room save timestamps and the encryption schema are not
changed. This is a display correction, not an assessment of drug indication.
Respiratory, neurologic and cardiovascular infusion formatting is unchanged.

Existing saved handoffs use the new grouping when reopened or exported. No
re-paste or remapping is needed solely for the new layout.

Install: save open work, extract PICU621.zip, open
PICU_Handoff_v6_21_DVT_Grouping, and replace the application repository files
with its contents. Keep app.py at the root and restart the deployment.
Keep Streamlit Secrets, password, encryption key, token and private data
repository unchanged. Do not run create_secrets.py again. No new dependencies
or storage-format changes are required. No direct GitHub changes were made.

See TEST_REPORT.md for checks and limitations. The preview is synthetic and
not a medication regimen or dosing recommendation.
